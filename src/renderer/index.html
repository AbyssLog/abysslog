About

AbyssLog tracks your EVE Online Abyssal Deadspace runs — timing, loot, consumption, losses, and statistics — so you can measure what's actually working and what's costing you ISK.

Runs are detected automatically via ESI when you enter and exit Abyssal Deadspace. Loot appraisals use Jita market prices via the Janice API. All data is stored locally on your machine.
